Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Do I book here or somewhere else?

Here. Every stay links to our live availability calendar, and the booking is confirmed directly with us — the same rooms, without the platform commission.

Is parking really free?

Yes — private parking in front of the house, included with every apartment and room, no reservation needed.

Can I cook?

All five apartments have their own kitchen. The two rooms (Room B and Deluxe Room 33) don't, but they share the terrace and are a short walk from restaurants in Bled.

What time can we arrive?

Tell us your arrival time when you book and we'll be there to hand over the keys. Late arrivals are usually no problem — just message us.

Which languages do you speak?

Slovene, English and German.

Not sure which one fits?

Tell us your dates and how many you are — we'll suggest the right stay and hold it for you.
