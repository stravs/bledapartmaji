The house

A vine-covered terrace, four apartments and three rooms.

Everything here is small on purpose. Each stay has its own entrance, so you come and go on your own schedule. Most have a kitchen; all have air conditioning and a place outside to sit.

We live on site — if you need a recommendation for a trail, a table, or a place to swim, knock or call.

The place

A lake, a gorge and the Karavanke behind them.

Walk the six kilometres around the water before breakfast. Row out to the island. Take the path into Vintgar gorge, or drive twenty minutes into the Bohinj valley and swim there instead.

Bled works in every season — quiet and blue in winter, green and slow in June. We'll tell you where the crowds aren't.
